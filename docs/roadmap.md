# Roadmap

Ideas under consideration for Foster Checkout. They are not commitments. To suggest one, open an issue.

- Save for later: move a line item out of the cart and back again.
- Shipping estimator: show an estimated shipping cost on the cart from a postal code.
- Custom checkout steps: add a step of the store's own to the checkout.
- Delivery date: show an estimated delivery date at checkout without a site template.
- Footer links in a modal: open the checkout's footer links in a modal on the page, so PCI compliance scans do not flag them as external links.
- More payment gateways.
- Mailchimp: subscribe newsletter sign-ups to a Mailchimp audience without a site module.
